JOB 1 - .env FIRST. THIS ORDER MATTERS.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Open C:\meet-the-patient\.env and change:
      TAVUS_INTERRUPTIBILITY=high
to:
      TAVUS_INTERRUPTIBILITY=low

Change nothing else in that file. Do not print the API key or the PIN.

Why: "high" means the patient stops talking the moment it hears anything
speech-like, which on a conference floor is constantly. Tavus's own best
practice is lower for most experiences. Last night the PALs were patched
to low directly on Tavus, but .env still said high, and the update in JOB 2
rebuilds PALs from .env. Editing .env first means the rebuild produces
PALs at low and the setting becomes permanent instead of being silently
reverted.

JOB 5 - AUDIO, the Zoom PodTrak P4next
----------------------------------------------------------------------
From the manual, checked last night:

- TAKE THE BATTERIES OUT. It is 2x AA, about 3.5 hours on alkaline. The
  manual also contradicts itself on which power source wins, and one
  reading has it sitting on battery and ignoring USB entirely. With no
  batteries in, that ambiguity cannot bite.
- Power: ideally a 5V USB mains adapter or power bank (12W / 5V 2.4A or
  better) into the DEDICATED POWER port, and the data cable to the PC.
  That keeps the P4 alive through PC reboots. If there is no adapter,
  the data cable alone bus-powers it and that is fine.
- Straight into a PC USB port. NOT a hub. Zoom's own troubleshooting.
- Set Auto Power Off to NEVER. Default is 10 hours.
- Phantom power 48V OFF. Broadcast headsets are dynamic, they do not
  need it, and 48V can damage gear that is not phantom-compatible.
- XLR is the MICROPHONE. 3.5mm is the HEADPHONES. Surgeon needs both.
  The observer's headset needs headphones only.
- In Windows Sound settings, set the P4next as BOTH default input AND
  default output. THIS IS THE ONE THAT SILENTLY BREAKS THINGS: the stand
  runs in live mode where the mic picker is hidden, so the app uses
  whatever Windows calls default.
- Turn off Windows USB selective suspend and sleep.
- Check the USB return channel is up in the headphone mix, or the surgeon
  will hear nothing from the patient and it will look like the app broke.

JOB 6 - THE TV, in this order
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The panel is a TCL running Google TV. There is no working remote: use the
single physical button on the TV. With the TV on, a SHORT press opens an
on-screen menu and further short presses step through the options.

  1. Rename the HDMI input this PC is on to "PC". On TCL Google TV that
     switches the panel to full 4:4:4 colour instead of 4:2:0. This is the
     single biggest picture improvement available and costs nothing.
  2. Picture mode to PC, Game or Monitor.
  3. Turn OFF: noise reduction, MPEG noise reduction, motion smoothing
     (action smoothing / MEMC), dynamic contrast, any "AI picture".
  4. Sharpness: try 0 and try mid, pick by eye on Margaret.

Also in Windows: display scaling at 100%, resolution 1080 x 1920, and in
Intel Graphics Command Center set output colour range to FULL, not Limited.
