JOB 6 - AUDIO. Zoom PodTrak P4next. From the manual, checked last night.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- TAKE THE BATTERIES OUT. 2x AA, about 3.5 hours on alkaline. The manual
  also contradicts itself on which power source wins, and one reading has
  it sitting on battery and ignoring USB. No batteries, no ambiguity.
- Power: a 5V USB mains adapter or power bank (12W / 5V 2.4A or better)
  into the DEDICATED POWER port, plus the data cable to the PC. That keeps
  it alive through PC reboots. Data cable alone also works.
- Straight into a PC USB port, NOT a hub. Zoom's own troubleshooting.
- Auto Power Off to NEVER. Default is 10 hours.
- Phantom power 48V OFF. Broadcast headsets are dynamic and do not need
  it, and 48V can damage gear that is not phantom-compatible.
- XLR is the MICROPHONE. 3.5mm is the HEADPHONES. Surgeon needs both.
  The observer needs headphones only.
- In Windows Sound settings make the P4next BOTH default input AND
  default output. THIS IS THE ONE THAT SILENTLY BREAKS THINGS: live mode
  hides the mic picker, so the app uses whatever Windows calls default.
  You can do this yourself in Settings.
- Turn off USB selective suspend and sleep.
- Check the USB return is up in the headphone mix, or the surgeon hears
  nothing from the patient and it looks like the app has broken.
